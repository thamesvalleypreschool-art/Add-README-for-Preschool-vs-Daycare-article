## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1. Is Preschool Better Than Daycare for Learning?

**Ans:** Preschool usually follows a planned learning curriculum, so it focuses more on early education and school readiness. Some daycare centres also offer planned learning activities. Parents should ask for the centre's daily routine and weekly activity plan.

### Q2. Do I Need Daycare If My Child Goes to Preschool?

**Ans:** Daycare is only necessary if preschool hours do not cover your working day. You can choose a preschool with extended daycare facilities, arrange separate daycare or find another suitable childcare option for the remaining hours.

### Q3. How Much Do Preschool and Daycare Cost in India?

**Ans:** Fees vary depending on the city, child's age, facilities and operating hours. There is no single fixed fee across India. Ask each centre for a written fee breakdown, including monthly charges, registration, meals, transport and late pickup fees. Compare the total yearly cost before enrolling.

### Q4. What Should I Check Before Choosing a Daycare?

**Ans:** Check the centre's safety arrangements, staff verification, caregiver-to-child ratio, hygiene, daily routine, illness policy and child pickup procedures. Visit the centre during operating hours and ask whether a trial day is available.

### Q5. Can a Daycare Prepare My Child for School?

**Ans:** A good daycare with planned activities can support children's language, social skills and independence. However, preschool or a preschool with daycare generally provides a more structured approach to early learning and school readiness.

## Your Next Step

Before choosing a preschool or daycare, write down your child's age and your actual working hours, including daily commuting time.

Then shortlist three centres and ask each one for its daily routine, facilities and fee structure in writing. Visit the centres, observe how staff interact with children and compare the overall environment before making your decision.

## Sources and Review

- **National Education Policy (NEP) 2020:** Ministry of Education, Government of India. It highlights the importance of early childhood care and education during the foundational stage, covering ages 3 to 8.
- **Daycare Registration:** Registration and licensing requirements may differ by state. Parents should ask each centre about its applicable registrations and permissions.
- **Fees and Timings:** Fees, operating hours and admission age limits vary from one centre to another.
