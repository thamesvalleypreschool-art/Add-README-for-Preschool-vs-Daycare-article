## Is Preschool and Daycare the Same Thing?

**No, preschool and daycare are not the same thing, although their services can overlap.** Preschool focuses on early learning and preparing children for school. Daycare focuses on providing care and supervision during the hours parents work. Some daycare centres offer learning activities, while some preschools provide extended care hours.

The best way to compare centres is to look beyond their names and understand what they actually offer. Parents should check the operating hours, daily routine, staff qualifications, childcare facilities and learning plans. Two centres using the same label may provide very different services.

### Key Differences to Consider

- **Purpose:** Preschool focuses on early education, while daycare focuses on childcare.
- **Timings:** Preschool usually offers shorter sessions, while daycare often provides longer hours.
- **Daily Routine:** Preschool follows planned learning activities, while daycare includes care, meals, rest and play.
- **Learning Approach:** Preschool generally follows a learning plan, while daycare activities vary by centre.
- **Staff:** Preschool usually has teachers, while daycare has caregivers and childcare staff.
