## What Are the Benefits of Preschool?

Preschool supports children's early development through planned, play-based activities. It helps children build important skills and become comfortable in a learning environment.

- **A Learning Routine:** Planned activities develop language, early number skills, movement and social interaction.
- **School Readiness:** Children become familiar with classrooms, teachers and group activities.
- **Friendships:** Children learn sharing, taking turns and cooperating with others.
- **Independence:** Everyday activities help children learn to wash their hands, eat snacks and manage their belongings.

## What Are the Benefits of Daycare?

Daycare provides a supportive environment for children while parents manage their work and daily responsibilities.

- **Longer, Reliable Hours:** Daycare schedules can accommodate working parents who need full-day childcare.
- **Care from a Young Age:** Many daycare centres accept infants and toddlers, depending on their facilities.
- **Daily Routine:** Regular meals, naps and playtime help children develop a consistent routine.
- **Social Interaction:** Children get opportunities to spend time with other children and caregivers.

## How Do You Decide Between Preschool and Daycare?

Choosing between preschool and daycare depends on your child's age, your family's schedule and the quality of the centre.

Ask yourself these three questions before making a decision:

1. **How Old Is My Child?** For younger children, daycare or a gentle playgroup may suit their needs. From around 3 years onwards, preschool can provide valuable early learning experiences.

2. **What Hours Do I Need?** If you work full-time, consider a preschool with extended daycare facilities or a daycare centre that offers a planned learning programme.

3. **What Does the Centre Actually Offer?** Visit the centre, check its facilities, ask for a written daily routine and, if available, arrange a trial visit.
