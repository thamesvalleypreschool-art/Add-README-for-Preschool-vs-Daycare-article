## Can a Child Attend Preschool and Daycare Together?

**Yes. Many children attend preschool in the morning and daycare in the afternoon, either at the same centre or at two different locations.** Some centres offer both services on one campus, making daily drop-offs easier for parents.

If you choose two different centres, consider how your child will travel between them, who will be responsible during each handover and whether both routines suit your child's needs.

Remember that children also need enough time to rest. Avoid making the daily schedule unnecessarily long or tiring.

### What Should Parents Consider?

- **Convenience:** Choose a location that makes daily travel manageable.
- **Safe Handover:** Confirm who is responsible for your child during transfers.
- **Daily Routine:** Make sure learning, meals, play and rest are balanced.
- **Child's Comfort:** Observe whether your child is adjusting well to the schedule.

## Does Daycare Provide Educational Activities?

**Many daycare centres provide educational activities, but the type and quality of activities vary.** Some centres plan songs, storytelling, art, creative play and outdoor activities. Others focus mainly on supervision, meals and rest.

Parents should ask for a written daily routine and a sample weekly activity plan before choosing a centre.

A good daycare routine usually includes:

- Indoor and outdoor play
- Stories, songs and conversations
- Drawing, painting and creative activities
- Age-appropriate learning games
- Healthy meals and rest periods

Look for caregivers who actively interact with children and encourage participation. Long periods of screen time or leaving children without meaningful activities may be a concern.

India's National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 emphasises play-based and activity-based learning during the early years. Therefore, daycare can also provide valuable learning experiences when activities are thoughtfully planned.
